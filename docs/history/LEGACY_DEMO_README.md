# ODYSSEY — demo cuts

## Primary: `odyssey-demo.mp4`

**64 seconds**, 1440x900, H.264 + AAC, narrated, with the original Odyssey score.
This is the cut embedded in the live site's Film section.

It tours the **Mission Control** experience:

Opening (8K Earth, waypoint rings, mission brief) → Initialize → boot/analysis → Analyze
→ Explore (science-layer dock, site callouts, live conditions) → Geology layer → Walk the
Site (3D terrain flythrough, drag-look, pause) → Time machine (seasonal sun, solar
declination, seasonal scores) → Campaign (animated route, Budget scenario) → command bar
("best geology") → final Budget campaign.

The voiceover is 63.8 s (`en-US-GuyNeural`, -3% rate, -2 Hz pitch); the music bed is the
original Odyssey score, ducked under the voice. The screen capture is a Playwright video
of the live app at 1440x900, with the page viewport forced to 1440x900 before recording.

## New narration

> NASA's Space Apps Challenge asks a hard question. Where on Earth can we test a permanent
> Moon base, or a Mars landing? No place is Mars. Every analog is partial. Odyssey starts
> with the experiment, not the planet. Define the mission. Odyssey decomposes it, scans
> thirty-nine candidate regions, and ranks them with three learned models. Explore the Earth
> as an instrument. Switch science layers, and the planet re-colors. Fly to a site. Walk a
> three-dimensional terrain built from its satellite frame. Move through time. See what Earth
> cannot provide. Then build the campaign. Three sites. Three experiments. Every leg priced.
> Thirty-nine sites. Three models. Four scenarios. One campaign. Odyssey. Your next mission
> starts on Earth.
