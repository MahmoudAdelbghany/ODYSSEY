# Target globe textures

Used for the Moon or Mars globe shown while an analysis runs. Equirectangular, 2048 × 1024, longitude −180° (left) to +180° (right).

**Moon:** `moon_lroc_2k.jpg`, NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (Ernie Wright), colour map from the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera WAC mosaic with polar fill. Public domain (NASA).
Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720 (`lroc_color_poles_2k.tif`, converted to JPEG).

**Mars:** `mars_viking_2k.jpg`, Mars Viking MDIM 2.1 colourised global mosaic (NASA/USGS, Viking Orbiter), retrieved as zoom-3 tiles from NASA Mars Trek (https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/) and stitched, then downsampled to 2048 × 1024. Public domain (NASA/USGS).
